Scale Lock

Pick a key. Every button plays a note in that key, so nothing sounds wrong.

Octave
Layout

    Shaded buttons are the key note (the tonic). The small number on each button is its place in the scale, 1 to 7.

    Computer keyboard: bottom row Z to comma, middle row A to K, top row Q to I.

    No sound on an iPhone? Flip the silent switch on the side to off.
